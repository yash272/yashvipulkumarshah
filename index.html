THE DECISION / SWITCHON
SWITCHON / PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

A product chapter
in manufacturing AI.

SwitchOn builds AI-powered quality inspection software for manufacturers and recently raised an $8M pre-Series B. I worked there as an Associate Product Manager, helping turn customer problems into shipped features.

How I worked

I spoke with customers and sales and marketing teams to understand the problem, wrote the PRD, and worked with designers, developers, and AI engineers to get the product built.

A feature I owned

One project was a new dimensional measurement tool for factory operators. The product had to help them prepare images, classify good and bad examples, and test whether a model could spot defects before cameras were deployed on an assembly line.

The product decision

Experienced operators were comfortable with the existing anomaly-detection workflow, so changing the interface created a real learning curve. We made the flow step by step, added guidance and responsive feedback, and let operators go back to find mistakes.

What happened

It shipped. Customers eventually liked the new flow. I observed that new operators were easier to train, mistakes were easier to find, and the overall process took less time as accuracy improved.

These are qualitative observations, not measured before-and-after metrics. The illustration on this page is a reconstruction of the concept; the linked product image is public material from SwitchOn.

THE LESSON / BOOKCHANGE
BOOKCHANGE

People before
the platform.

I founded a peer-to-peer book-sharing platform. I built the product before I had built a focused community around it.

What worked

Books and Brews showed me how much better the idea felt when readers were actually together. It was a genuinely good experience. Bookchange also reached the final audition stage of Shark Tank India.

What didn't

I was unfocused, and I hadn't built a committed community on one side of the marketplace. The meetup came after six months, when I'd run out of money.

What I'd change

I'd bring a small, specific community together first. Make it easy for those people to exchange books, learn what keeps them coming back, then build around that behaviour.

Final audition stage means an audition milestone, not a televised pitch or finalist placement.
